Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find Padel courts near me?

Visit our Clubs & Locations page and use the search feature. You can also contact us directly for personalized recommendations.

What equipment do I need to start playing?

Check out our comprehensive Equipment Guide for detailed recommendations based on your skill level and budget.

Can complete beginners play Padel?

Absolutely! Padel is very beginner-friendly. Most clubs offer beginner sessions, and you can start enjoying the game from your very first lesson.
